A letter, just for you

For my beloved, Amira

November 11

My love,

I want to start this by letting you know that I love you very dearly. My love for you soars through the clouds and into the night skies that we both share. It pours more than the rainiest of rainfalls, and shines brighter than any light that falls into your eyes. Loving you from afar has been difficult for me. Moving hasn’t been great as you can see very clearly. I’m homesick. I’m missing home, you. And it’s taking a toll on me like nothing has ever in so long. It’s probably really bad and selfish but I find myself clinging on to you more than ever. It hits me really hard when I don’t have you around. It pains me. I’ve never longed for someone the way I have and am for you. Your absence leaves me hollow. Though I know you have a life of your own to live, things to do and I can’t follow you everywhere. I’ve been really selfish. I’ve been hurting and I’ve poured my sorrows onto you. I have gone about things in a horrible way, I’m really not proud of. I’ve been something to you that I’ve not wanted to be. A burden (or something along those lines). I have been really unappreciative, really misunderstanding, hard to reach out, and just worse things piled up. I do want to tell you, even though at times it may not seem like it, your presence does wonders to me. It saves me. Though I don’t say it directly, it’s all that keeps me going. I find myself just wanting more and when I know this is all I have, it hurts me. Everything you say is right, and I am aware of it all more than anyone. But my love, it’s hard for me to accept, hard to start anew. I am trying. I haven’t been loving you right, I haven’t been good to you. I want to carry on, but it’s been difficult. I’m taking one day at a time. Usually it's the loneliness that gets to me. Everyone has everyone, and I had no one of the sort. The daytime eats me alive. I seem to be stopping myself subconsciously. As of now, carrying on almost sounds like betrayal. Like I'm letting go. I love you, no matter what I feel. I love you, no matter how bad the day is. I love you, no matter what comes in between us. I may feel a lot of things, some that aren’t good too. But that never collides with what I truly feel for you. It hurts me, seeing you hurt. It’s the last thing I want. Through all the pain, the only other thing I feel is the love I have for you, and the love you have for me. I realize that you make it so easy to love you, with all the things you do for me. All my dreams are memories of us, memories of you, your voice and face. I want to apologize darling, your love means everything to me. You mean everything to me. I promise I’m trying, and I don’t know what that means yet but I’m trying. Please bear with me, be patient while I get through my worst times, while I can’t seem to get my words right, while I slip up. You’re all I need and I’m trying to get by with what I have love and it’s been hard. I love you, and I want you more than anything. I love you darling, I love you most. I want to be there. I want you here. I want you. I need you. Your beauty is the essence of my soul, the light in my eyes, the spark in my heart., and I never want to lose that.

I love you, amira

Always.

Ayyub
